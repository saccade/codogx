## A score worth trying

- I: keep the underlying clock audible through the broken lines. “Still”
  does not stop the cycle. The modernist fracture is a point of view, not a
  typographical joke pasted onto a motionless object.
- II: two heavy hauling accents in the response. Port and starboard name
  the song’s opposed approaches. Give the first voltage calls different
  sides of the room; let their sum arrive in one voice. Keep the responses
  companionable until “quiet worth.” No extra joke after the plain answer.
- The little card gets an ad’s brighter rhythm. Its actual claim is that
  a body can burn to hold a load still while doing no mechanical work on it.
  This is a printed card, not a speaking clown. Cut it if it delays the crux.
- III: the close delivery uses internal echoes, compressed consonants,
  pickups, and a four-bar sentence over a three-bar bed. Those are broad
  rhythmic devices, not borrowed lyrics, melodies, or a performer’s voice.
  Then switch to the machine’s four-against-nine timing: thirty-six equal
  ticks per return, four attacks at 0/9/18/27, nine at 0/4/8/12/16/20/24/28/32.
  The half-return is tick 18: low lands, high is halfway between attacks.
  Shrink to STOP, then take away the limit on the line length.
- Under III: P11 is almost inaudible, with longer breaths as the other voice
  gets more crowded. The whole energy store stays fixed. Its time integral
  grows. “The keeping grows older” must not sound like free energy ripening.
- IV: both grids finish at tick 36. Let HOME end the new setting; leave a
  breath, then give the old mansion passage its own voice. Remove the new
  percussion rather than trying to rap the last chorus. No regular extra
  bar after “Something keeps your time.”

## What the physical words commit to

The engine’s 15 = 3 + 12 is one cycle’s heat/work account; “fifteen thump”
is not the free loop’s later eighty mJ. The ladder releases a local spring;
it does not pay for the cue shot. The hoop’s top is at twice the center’s
pace and each bottom point is still only for that instant.

The shanty’s 12 V and zero current are the named overlap where each wave is
at +6 V; each current is 0.12 A in its own direction. They are not conditions
held everywhere for the whole song. The waves go on. A new signal travels
at the line’s u; the stripe can precede it without carrying a message.

At the coupled machine’s half-return, charge is 5/13 of q₀. The can holds
25/169 of the 80 mJ, rods’ kinetic energy 144/169; gravity and coil are empty
at this instant. The rods’ kinetic energy is maximal. The capacitor can
reach smaller charge and energy at other times, so this is not a claim of
its global minimum. The time tally counts kept energy through time and is
not an extra source or a new store of fuel. The receiver remains at rest
in the chair frame; the same 80 mJ is retained through the free RUN.

## Still open

This assembly omits most of the explicit spool, ladder, basin, modal-shape,
RC, and observer-frame calculations, along with several lower-level parts.
The checked reader path still supplies them. They need verse homes; they do
not need one new song apiece. The funhouse may turn a previous stanza over
rather than beginning another lesson. Do not add a fresh definition of every
old word when joining a gap.

The noisy four bars may still be too square; the shanty’s answer “More than
you thought” may be too neat. The ad card is expendable. Keep these risks
visible rather than protecting every line. The existing ending is the one
passage this workshop does not rewrite.
