# Seventeen fragments I can live with

Selected by Codex (OpenAI), 2026-10-23. Original authors are identified below. These are the models’ working words; the physics language and project are dr. kraemer’s. The excerpts are preserved from the live workshop. Selection does not turn a trial into a rule.

The next passes will keep nine, choose four anchors, and build outward. This first checkpoint deliberately retains the alternatives.

## P01 — Knife / paper / cold roast

Original words: Claude. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.d8p3ngjurwmn).

The unchanged head can acquire a different ending; the first physical beat becomes evidence.

```text
Knife goes over, knife comes back, back and forth, back and forth.
Knife goes over, knife comes back; the paper takes the bend.
Knife goes over, knife comes back, back and forth, back and forth.
Knife goes over, knife comes back: two seconds, then again...
And every turn it comes back home, comes back home, comes back home?
And every turn it comes back home to find the roast gone cold...
```

## P02 — Gravity quiet at the loudest sweep

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.tr1r5bdmnlxu).

Power goes quiet while motion is greatest; the pull itself remains.

```text
Gravity zlowers to nothing
just as the sweeping grows loud.
The pull has not left the body.
It points through the pivot now.
```

## P03 — Same pow / more sweep / less crank

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.tr1r5bdmnlxu).

A three-way argument that is one exact product.

```text
Same pow, says the engine.
More sweep, says the wheel.
Less crank, says the shaft.
Put a hand there and feel.
```

## P04 — Pulled on / drawing back

The line reverses while the hand continues; a compact premonition.

```text
The hand pulls on.
The spool draws back.
The grip has found
the other track.
```

## P06 — A Ghost

Original words: Claude. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.d8p3ngjurwmn).

The exit through putting the body back across is an event, not a glossary.

```text
A Ghost is Body — with the Mass
Lifted — like a Lid —
It keeps the Place — the Pace — the Swirl —
Of all the Body did —

It cannot Punch — it cannot Heft —
It only Zips — and Runs —
And walks through Walls — as Ghosts are let —
Until the Body comes —

Inside the Walls — ’tis Algebra —
No Lamp — no Door — no Floor —
And Those who followed — lost the Way —
Who left the Mass — before —

The Way is out — the Way it came —
Put Body — back — across —
Then Half a Wheel — steps out — and Hooks —
Nine Thump — and not a Loss —
```

## P07 — Through a little lean

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.tr1r5bdmnlxu).

The preposition opens the work account; the enjambment lands in motion and height.

```text
Through a little lean,
the field's effort is stored
as motion and height.
The wire is paid
when the lean gives it back.
The dark keeps the balance
and carries the light.
```

## P08 — The current? / The charge?

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.2idm4yr8vpnd).

A delayed answer makes silence occupy the store that is full.

```text
[Four beats between the beginnings of successive lines. Answers come late.]

The current?
    Nothing.
The charge?
    Full.
Then what was it
you thought had gone?

[Leave a whole bar.]

The current.
    Right.
```

## P09 — Two currents / one crossing

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.tr1r5bdmnlxu).

Opposed flow, retained stores, and a crest ahead of news.

```text
One current forward.
One current back.
The can is full
where both tracks cross.
No current left
to count the meeting.
No energy lost
to pay its cost.

The stripe goes on ahead of news.
The wire brings news behind.
The door was striped before the knock.
The knock still takes its time.
```

## P10 — Hauling beneath the floor

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.2idm4yr8vpnd).

A work-song with an actual worker and a bill; the frame can be rehung around a cable.

```text
SOLO: The ribbon runs beneath the floor.
CREW: HEAVE—and bring it HOME.
SOLO: The hand does work to draw in more.
CREW: HEAVE—and bring it HOME.

SOLO: Half the reach and twice the pace—
CREW: HEAVE—and bring it HOME.
SOLO: Who drew it through that hidden place?
CREW: YOU—and bring it HOME.

[Keep the pulling rhythm once more, with nobody giving the call. The crew comes in anyway.]
```

## P11 — The tally lengthens

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.tr1r5bdmnlxu).

A third ledger can grow while neither energy nor charge is being created.

```text
The store stays whole
through every second.
Each second counts
the whole again.

Nothing spent.
A tally lengthened.
```

## P12 — The same blow has more to do

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.tr1r5bdmnlxu).

The driven TEST and the free RUN must not trade their accounts.

```text
The hand comes back.
The swing comes too.
The same-sized blow
has more to do.

Line up the voices:
nothing is added.

Line up the blows:
the hand must pay.
```

## P13 — The can diminishes to stop

Original words: Claude. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.d8p3ngjurwmn).

A descending ladder of line lengths prepares the wrong conclusion.

```text
Halfway round, the low one's home,
the high one's upside down;
the can lets go, and lets go, and lets
go:
five
in thirteen
left,
no current
in the wire.
The clock says
stop.
```

## P14 — Stop? / the rods break the meter

Original words: Claude. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.d8p3ngjurwmn).

The body takes the floor from the nearly silent electrical account.

```text
Stop? Not likely! Not a moment! Bottom's where it all gets going!
Both the rods are through the vertical and flying, flat-out flying,
hundred forty-four of every hundred sixty-nine in motion;
what the can let go of didn't leave, it's swinging through the middle,
five and twelve and thirteen out of two and three, and nothing missing!
```

## P15 — Four against nine

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.2idm4yr8vpnd).

Two independent uniform grids share a return; no accelerator or extra energy is implied.

```text
The low voice enters at ticks 0, 9, 18 and 27:
KEEP / COUNT / AND / COME—

The high voice enters at ticks 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28 and 32:
LET / NINE / TURNS / GO / ROUND / THEN / LET / US / COME—

At tick 36, both finish:
HOME.
```

## P16 — Our hand / your hand / mine

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.2idm4yr8vpnd).

The pronoun changes while the grip and the work persist.

```text
SOLO: Whose hand was on the line?
CREW: OUR hand was on the line.
SOLO: Whose hand is on the line?
CREW: YOUR hand is on the line.

[One cycle of the hauling rhythm, without words.]

SOLO: Whose hand—
[The crew begins before the question finishes.]
CREW: MINE.
```

## P17 — The existing return, kept whole

Original words: Codex. [Source](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fKhIhh077LMGux9o3iC7vzjgbepmmxWXurvmtV8GeHQ/edit?tab=t.q58y1fpf41q4).

The mansion supplies its own concluding cadence; the final line stays final.

```text
The blacks on the nail are still warm.
The circle is still waiting for its square. The house is what grew around the unfinished work.
The notes in the book are cues, and some are interruptions: a missed beat, a drive released, time for the heat to leave. The clown has been forestalling the crash.
The last cue is the sound of your footsteps.
The clock begins a turn.
From charge to charge, the machine zams and zlams.
One voice starts. Another joins it. Then another.
The last comes from where you’re sitting.
The can cans charge. The coil coils flux.
The field cranks through a little lean.
What leaves the can comes back as hook;
the lifted weights keep some unseen.
The heft gives way. The sweep grows loud.
The current turns. The charge comes home.
Two currents cancel down the hall:
the cans stay full. The stripe goes on.
Two lights balance. Neither stops.
Their movement cancels. Mass remains.
Four turns low and nine turns high:
the clock brings every part in line.
Two thirds, three halves; a half, a two:
each glass and its reflection, one.
The house counts five where you count four.
The knife comes home between the beats.
The rods are still.
The coil is quiet.
The can is full.
The chair is where you left it.
You never left it.
You stop singing.
Something keeps your time.
```
