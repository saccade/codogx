## B — The crew in the hatbox

Codex; new wire version of the earlier work-song frame.

Possible place: The hall’s striped hatbox, Drawer f; its line and two tuning forks lead naturally into Mirror 3.

Surface / interior: Two hauling accents per response; port and starboard first sing apart, then arrive together. At the named overlap, 6 V plus 6 V gives 12 V; the opposed 0.12 A currents cancel. Both traveling waves retain their energy. A crest can precede a message; a new signal still travels with the wave.

Setting: The common response is companionable at first. Remove the regular response after the question about quiet; the next voice comes from too close. Port and starboard are the song’s two opposed approaches, not directions secretly attached to a page.

```text
PORT:      Six volts aboard from port, away—
STARBOARD: Six volts from starboard, clear—
TOGETHER:  Twelve on the can where the currents meet,
           and not an ampere here.

CALL:      Has either wave been eaten?
CREW:      Both pass with all they brought.
CALL:      Then what was all that quiet worth?
CREW:      More than you thought.

CALL:      That stripe was there before the knock?
CREW:      The waves were there before.
CALL:      Then who brings word of casting off?
CREW:      The wave—from hand to door.
```

## C — The false stop

Claude’s shrinking charge and long-line reversal; Codex’s four opening bars and quiet counter-account.

Possible place: 13-c and Appendix γ, α-3/α-4: the same machine and the same halfway instant, seen from another account.

Surface / interior: Dense internal echoes prepare a staircase of shorter lines. Stop is seized by a suddenly overfull line. At T/2, i=0 and the rods pass vertical at maximum kinetic energy. Charge is 5/13 of q₀; the can holds 25/169 and the rods 144/169 of the total. This is not a claim that the capacitor’s charge or energy never goes lower at another time. The whole free RUN retains 80 mJ.

Setting: Use a close, syncopated four-bar delivery over a three-bar bed for the opening only; the mismatch is a speech device, not an assertion of the mode ratio. Then let the low/faster voices use the actual four-against-nine grid over one return. At its halfway point the low grid lands and the high is halfway between returns. Keep P11’s tally almost inaudible beneath the rush.

## D — The mansion takes the room back

Codex; existing protected ending, unchanged.

Possible place: The existing Ending 4, last.

Surface / interior: The extra rhythmic apparatus leaves; the house’s measured stresses return. The can comes home full; rods and coil are quiet; the receiver is at rest in the chair’s frame. The same kept account and own time complete the visit.

Setting: Do not overlay the fast syllables on this chorus. Let one voice enter the old cadence, then others, and let them leave unevenly. No prose or new verse follows its last line.

```text

The circle is still waiting for its square. The house is what grew around the unfinished work.
The notes in the book are cues, and some are interruptions: a missed beat, a drive released, time for the heat to leave. The clown has been forestalling the crash.
```
